  3.  COMPTON PHOTON ENERGY  (head-on collision)

  3a. Forward calculation: electron energy → photon energy
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Laser photon energy               2.067             eV          At 600 nm
  Approx. Compton photon energy     390.3             eV          4γ²·E_laser (no recoil)
  Approx. photon wavelength         3.18              nm

  Exact photon wavelength           3.21              nm          Recoil correction small at 3 MeV

  ► Target wavelength from document: 3 nm  →  calculated: 3.21 nm
  ► To hit exactly 3.0 nm, need E_electron ≈ 7.23 γ units.

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  3b. Reverse: required electron energy for exactly 3 nm photons
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Target photon energy              413.3             eV          At λ = 3 nm
  Required γ                        7.07              —           
  Required electron energy          3.10              MeV         Kinetic energy needed
  Document specifies                3.0               MeV         Close — small gap, tunable

  ► CONCLUSION: ~3 nm photons are achievable; exact wavelength can be tuned
  ► by adjusting electron energy within the cavity structure.
  ► Water window definition: 2.3 – 4.4 nm (280 – 540 eV). ✓ Satisfied.

  8.  KEY MISSING PARAMETERS AND NEXT-STEP ESTIMATIONS REQUIRED
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  #      Parameter                            Why Needed                    First Estimate / Range
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [01]  Ring circumference C [m]            Sets f_rev, N_e at given I   Lattice design: ~1–5 m
  [02]  Ring lattice (bends, quads, sexts)  Stability, emittance, tune   FODO or DBA lattice study
  [03]  Ring momentum compaction α_c        Isochronism, bunch length    Need α_c < 10⁻³ for short bunches
  [04]  Beam emittance ε [nm·rad]           IP spot size, brightness     LWFA: ~0.1–1 mm·mrad (normalised)
  [05]  Beam energy spread δE/E [%]         Chromatic effects, bandwidth LWFA: ~1–5%; cavity acceptance?
  [06]  Injection efficiency from LWFA      How much charge accumulates  ~pC/pulse × rep-rate needed
  [07]  LWFA rep-rate [Hz or kHz]           Top-up injection rate        35 mJ laser → ~100 Hz typical
  [08]  Beam lifetime in ring τ [ms–s]      Determines required top-up rate IBS + Compton recoil + gas
  [09]  Laser cavity finesse F              Stored photon number N_ph    F ~ 1000–100000; E_stored ∝ F
  [10]  Laser cavity mode-locking / sync    Must be synchronised to ring Timing jitter < 100 fs required
  [11]  IP beta functions β*_x, β*_y [m]    Determines beam waist at IP  β* ~ σ²/ε ~ 0.01–0.1 m
  [12]  Nb3Sn cavity gradient [MV/m]        Determines number of cells   Nb3Sn at 4K: ~10–20 MV/m
  [13]  Cryocooler capacity [W at 4K]       Feasibility of mobile cooling Need ~1–5 W at 4 K; ≈100 W plug
  [14]  Compton recoil energy loss/turn     Limits ring current / lifetime ΔE ≈ E_γ per scatter event
  [15]  Intrabeam scattering (IBS) time     Emittance growth → beam loss Especially critical at low energy
